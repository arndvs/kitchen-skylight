# Fluffy Pancakes
tags: breakfast, easy, kid-friendly
servings: 4
prep: 5
cook: 10

## Ingredients
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 cup milk
- 1 egg

## Directions
1. Whisk the dry ingredients in a bowl.
2. Whisk in the milk and egg until just combined — a few lumps are fine.
3. Rest the batter 5 minutes while the griddle heats.
4. Cook on a hot, buttered griddle until bubbles form, then flip and cook until golden.
